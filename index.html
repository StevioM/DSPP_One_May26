BSc coursework · Alteryx

Do Pokemon stat archetypes actually exist?

"The legendary tier is not legendary." One of the findings, from a model that was never told which Pokemon were legendary.

K-means clustering of 386 Generation 1-3 Pokemon on their six base stats, with no type, generation or legendary flag given to the model. Four of the five archetypes competitive players recognise came back out of the numbers alone. The validity measures preferred k = 2 over the k = 5 the theory predicted, which is reported rather than tidied away. The workflow, every tool setting and the result tables are all in the repo, so the numbers can be checked without Alteryx.
